Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

TacticalPawns is a personal tool used only by its creator with his own TikTok account.

Data accessed

Through TikTok Login Kit (scope user.info.basic) the app reads basic profile information of the authorizing account (the owner). Through the Content Posting API (scope video.upload) it uploads videos to the owner's inbox.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are kept privately by the owner and are never shared, sold or used for advertising. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
